I'm The Guy, and today, we will talk about **Luna**, Earth's moon.
> 我是The Guy，今天我们要聊的，是地球的卫星——**月球**。

The moon is the oldest human colony outside Earth, and hosts ancient settlements stretching back thousands of years of history.
> 月球是地球外最古老的人类殖民地，拥有延续数千年的古老定居点。

As you know, **Luna** is the only natural satellite and closest celestial body to Earth.
> 如你所知，**月球**是地球唯一的天然卫星，也是距离地球最近的天体。

Since it is prominent in Earth's night sky, **Luna** was a symbol of great significance in the culture of early man, and it was gazed upon in awe and wonder for thousands of years.
> 由于在地球的夜空中格外醒目，**月球**在早期人类文化中具有非凡的象征意义；数千年来，人类一直以敬畏与惊叹的目光仰望着它。

However, it was only until the 4th century before **Tranquility** that they began to figure out the true nature of the large pale circle in their sky.
> 然而，直到**静海历**前四世纪，他们才开始探明天空中那轮白玉盘的本质。

Humans first landed on the moon on July 20th, 1969 **Common Era**.
> 人类于**公元**1969年7月20日首次登上月球。

Many centuries later, the exact time the first words on the moon were spoken would be adopted as Year Zero for a new calendar, the **After Tranquility** calendar.
> 许多世纪之后，人类在月球上说出第一句话的精确时刻，被定为新历法——**静海历**的零年。

In 68 **After Tranquility**, infrastructure began to be constructed by remote-controlled robots called **Telenots**.
> 在**静海历**68年，被称为**Telenots**的远程控制机器人开始（在月球上）建设基础设施。

Human colonization on the moon began in 91 **A.T.**, and the largest settlement, **Artemis**, held 200 people by 119 **A.T.**
> 人类于月球上的殖民活动始于91**A.T.**，直到119**A.T.**，最大的定居点**Artemis**已有200名居民。

Later, an equatorial mass driver was constructed on the lunar equator.
> 之后，月球赤道上建成了一座赤道质量投射器。

The driver consisted of railroads delivering iron, titanium, aluminum, oxygen, and other goods to launching ramps where they would be shot into space.
> 这套投射器由轨道系统构成，将铁、钛、铝、氧以及其他货物运至发射坡道，再将其弹射入太空中。

The materials mined on **Luna** would be used to build many spacecraft.
> 从**月球**上开采出的材料将被用于建造大量航天器。

The mass driver in the colonies were powered by solar-powered collectors made of black silicon or aluminum mirrors.
> 殖民地中的质量投射器由太阳能采集器供能，这些收集器由黑硅或铝制反射镜制成。

A swarm of spacecraft at the Earth-Sun L1 point helped deliver extra power to Earth and **Luna**, and it also helped propel vessels using massive particles like ions.
> 位于地日L1点的航天器群向地球和月球输送额外能量，并利用离子等具质量粒子协助飞船推进。

After hundreds of years, many cities were built on Earth's moon.
> 数百年后，地球之月已然城市林立。

**Luna** developed a rich history and culture after thousands of years of habitation.
> 数千年的栖居之后，**月球**逐渐积淀了丰富的历史与文化。

The culture on **Luna** is a blend of **Nearbaseline Human**, **Superior Human**, these being augmented humans, and AI culture.
> 月球上的文化，融合了**近基准人类**与**超人类**两种增强人类文化，以及人工智能文化。

After **the Great Expulsion**, many refugees came streaming in from Earth.
> **大驱逐**之后，大量来自地球的大量难民涌入月球。

Despite losses from **the Technocalypse**, **Luna** eventually recovered and thrived, even becoming the capital of the solar system sometimes.
> 尽管在**技术灾变**中遭受重创，**月球**最终还是恢复了元气、重新走向繁荣，甚至一度成为太阳系的首都。

In **the Current Era**, the side facing Earth has been modified to appear in a relatively pristine state, as per **GAIA**'s orders.
> 在本纪元，应**GAIA**的要求，月之亮面受到改造，展现相对原始的状态。

The side facing away from Earth, meanwhile, has been allowed to develop further, with multiple **worldhouses**, underground settlements, observation domes, and maglev railways.
> 与此同时，月之暗面则被允许继续发展，拥有多个**行星天幕**、地下定居点、观测穹顶和磁悬浮轨道。
