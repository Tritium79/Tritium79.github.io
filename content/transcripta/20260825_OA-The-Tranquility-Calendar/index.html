[译稿][待整理]何为静海历（Orion's Arm Universe Project）

25 Aug. 2026 / 丙午年 丙申月 辛未日

In the EG, and in these videos, you will often see dates labeled as AT or BT.

在《银河百科全书》以及本频道视频中，你会经常看到以 AT 或 BT 记载的日期。

So, what do these dates mean?

那么，这些日期代表什么呢？

These dates are from the Tranquility Calendar.

这些日期来自“静海历”。

It is a calendar that is used widely throughout the Terragen Sphere,

这是一种在整个地源地带广泛使用的历法，

even though it is not the most common system as the Terragen Sphere is vast.

尽管由于地源地带幅员辽阔，它并不是最常用的历法系统。
tips：并非指“静海历是一个冷门历法”，而是指“静海历是一种通用历法，但人们倾向于使用本地（恒星系、行星系）历法”

Even though it is not the most common, it is used in our version of the EG.

尽管它并非最常用，但我们版本的《银河百科全书》使用的正是这套历法。

So, we will need to have a grasp of it.

因此，我们应当对其有所了解。

Another quick thing to remember before we get started

在开始之前，还要记住一点：

is that due to relativistic time effects, specific dates and times are mostly approximate.

由于相对论效应，具体的日期和时间大多只能视为近似值。

But now, let's get started. First up, what are BT and AT?

但现在，让我们开始吧。首先，BT 和 AT 是什么？

BT stands for Before Tranquility and AT stands for After Tranquility.

BT 是 静海前（Before Tranquility）的缩写，AT 则是 静海后（After Tranquility）的缩写。

What does the word tranquility mean?

那么tranquility（静海） 是什么意思呢？

The word tranquility refers to the first words ever spoken by Terragens from another world outside Earth.

tranquility指的是地源体首次抵达地外世界后说出的第一句话。

When humans first landed on the moon, the first words that were spoken were "Houston, Tranquility Base here. The Eagle has landed."

人类首次登上月球时，说出的第一句话是：“休斯顿，这里是静海基地，‘鹰’着陆成功。”
tips：https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history//alsj/a11/a11transcript_tec.html、
https://www.nasa.gov/history/mission-transcripts-mercury-gemini-and-apollo/、https://www.chinanews.com.cn/gj/gj-sjkj/news/2009/07-15/1776258.shtml、https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%9D%99%E6%B5%B7%E5%9F%BA%E5%9C%B0

The exact middle of the moment the word tranquility was spoken is the central date for the Tranquility Calendar.

说出“tranquility”一词的时间中点，就是静海历的中心基准时刻。
登月舱着陆时间为1969年7月20日20:17:40(UTC)，阿姆斯特朗踏上月表时间为1969年7月21日02:56:20(UTC)
根据NASA档案，此时间中点应处于1969年07月20日20:17:59(UTC)到1969年07月20日20:18:04(UTC)之间

Before this point, it's labeled as BT, and after this point is AT.

这一时刻之前记为 BT，之后则记为 AT。

The exact center day in the calendar is known as Moon Landing Day, and it is not counted as part of a month or year.

历法之中，此精确中心日被称为登月日，不计入任何月份或年份。
tips：登月日是历史上的一个唯一日期，而不是每年固定出现的日期。

20 hours, 18 minutes and 1.2 seconds before the center point of the word tranquility, and 3 hours, 41 minutes and 58.8 seconds after the center point of the word tranquility are counted as part of Moon Landing Day.

从“tranquility”一词的中点之前 20 小时 18 分 1.2 秒，到该中点之后 3 小时 41 分 58.8 秒的这段时间，都计入登月日。

The rest of the calendar is structured so that a year is the same amount of time it takes for Earth to go around Sol.

余下部分，则以地球绕太阳运行一周的时间来定义一年。

Years have 13 months, each of which are named after famous scientists.

每年包含 13 个月，以著名科学家的名字命名月份。

The months are as follows,

Each month has exactly 28 days, and the first day of every month is always a Friday.

每个月恰好有 28 天，每月第一天总是星期五。

There is a special day called Armstrong Day, which is not part of any month, and it happens every year on the anniversary of Moon Landing Day.

此外，每年还设有一个名为“阿姆斯特朗日”的特殊日，它不属于任何月份，定于登月日的周年纪念日。

Finally, every four years, except for some exceptions, there is a day called Aldrin Day, which happens between Hippocrates 27 and Hippocrates 28. There are also some leap days every 400 years.

最后，除某些例外，每四年还会设有一个名为“阿尔德林日”的特殊日，它位于希波克拉底月27日与28日之间。每 400 年也有一些闰日。
tips：Aldrin，登月第二人，阿姆斯特朗的队友

And that is basically the Tranquility Calendar. Thanks for your watching and goodbye, explorers!
