Subatomic particles, these are amazing little things that are everywhere.

亚原子粒子——这些奇妙的微小存在遍布各处。

Imagine if there were no subatomic particles, we would be dead, we would cease to exist.

试想一下，倘若没有亚原子粒子，我们将不复存在、彻底消逝于世间。

However, there are certain types of subatomic particles that we don't need to survive, but are very useful in modern technology, such as magnetic monopoles.

然而，某些亚原子粒子并非我们生存所必需，却在现代技术中大有用处，例如磁单极子。

What are these magnetic monopoles?

何为磁单极子？

First, we have to know what monopoles even are.

首先，我们需要了解，何为单极子。

Monopoles are like knots in the fabric of the universe, which formed when the universe was cooling.

单极子如同编织宇宙结构时产生的纽结，随宇宙的冷却而诞生。
tips：The Fabric of the Cosmos是一部纪录片，中译名宇宙的结构，故“Fabric”译为“结构”。

Imagine you have a pot of molten lava and it cools very fast.

设想一下，有一坩埚熔岩正在快速冷却。
tips：lava是“熔岩”，magma是“岩浆”，“熔岩”or“岩浆”？你认知里的Minecraft译名科学吗？【SkyEye_FAST】。

Crystalline structures start forming in different regions inside the pot.

坩埚内不同区域开始形成晶体结构。

The structures trigger nearby atoms in the fluid to also settle down and attach into the pattern.

这些晶体结构促使熔体中邻近原子也附着其上、嵌入该晶格排列中。

However, when different crystallizing regions meet, they are each crystallizing in a different way and orientation, and they are cooling too fast to connect with each other.

然而，当不同结晶区相遇时，由于各自的结晶方式和晶体取向不同，且冷却速率过快，使得它们无法彼此衔接。

This creates separation points between the different regions, each with different disconnected patterns.

这导致各区域之间形成晶界，各自具有互不相连、互不相同的晶格排列。

These patterns would have different crystalline structures, and so you would have to remelt the whole pot in order to fix the defects.

由于这些晶格排列具有不同的晶体取向，因此必须将整坩埚的熔体彻底重熔，才能消除这些缺陷。
tips：这一部分不知所云，此段在描述晶粒和晶界的形成，但混淆了“晶体结构”Crystal structure与“晶体取向”Crystal orientation。另外，Crystal pattern根本没有标准译法，此处替换为“晶格排列”。除此之外，在此直接使用译者所掌握的晶体学语言，对本段的某些部分进行了重述。

Now imagine this pot is the entire universe,

接下来，想象这口坩埚便是整个宇宙，

and when the universe cooled billions of years ago, it was separated into different regions, which caused defects in the fabric of spacetime.

在数十亿年前宇宙冷却之时，它被分隔成不同的区域，因而在时空结构中产生了缺陷。

It caused defects in something called the Higgs field.

具体而言，是在名为“希格斯场”的结构中产生了缺陷。

So what even is the Higgs field?

那么，何为希格斯场？

Well, imagine you are swimming through a viscid fluid with other people.

设想一下，你和其他人正在一种黏性流体中游动。

Some people have more trouble swimming through it than others.

一些人穿行其中要比另一些人费力得多。

The Higgs field is like this fluid, and it is everywhere in the universe.

希格斯场就如同这种流体，充斥于整个宇宙之中。

The more trouble you have swimming through it, the more mass you have.

穿行时所受的阻滞越大，你所具有的质量也就越大。

This is how objects in the universe have mass.

宇宙中的物体便是这样具有质量的。

And so monopoles are basically defects in this field.

因此，单极子本质上就是该场中形成的缺陷。

There are different types of monopoles.

单极子有着不同的类型。

Some are known as dyons, which carry both electric and magnetic charge.

有些同时携带电荷与磁荷，被称为双荷子。
tips：双荷子，Julian Seymour Schwinger提出的物理概念，是四维理论中同时具有电荷和磁荷的假想粒子。

Some monopoles can even be as massive as an amoeba.

有些单极子的质量甚至大到与一只变形虫相当。

Monopoles can stick to protons or neutrons and then break them apart, releasing a positron and turning the proton or neutron into a special type of particle called a pion.

单极子能够附着于质子或中子上，并将它们瓦解，释放出一个正电子，同时将质子或中子转化为名为π介子的粒子。

But we are here to talk about a special type of monopole, which is known as Magnetic Monopole.

而我们要探讨的，是一种特殊的单极子——磁单极子。

These are monopoles which carry only magnetic charge.

这是一种仅携带磁荷的单极子。

What does it mean when we say they carry magnetic charge?

那么，何为“携带磁荷”？

Well, imagine cutting a bar magnet in half.

不妨设想一下，将一根条形磁铁切成两半。

When you do this, you will not get a north side and a south side, instead you will get two separate magnets, each with their own north and south poles.

这样操作并不能得到孤立的北极与南极，相反，你只会得到两块独立的磁铁，各自拥有北极与南极。

But what if there's a particle that was only north or south?

但倘若存在某种粒子，只具有北极或南极呢？

These are magnetic monopoles.

此即磁单极子。

They carry units of magnetic charge, and a north monopole will annihilate a south monopole, kind of like matter and antimatter.

它们携带着单位磁荷，北单极子与南单极子会相互湮灭，宛如物质与反物质的湮灭。

Monopoles are like tiny bombs which can catalyze fusion reactions and create explosions, so there cannot be that many monopoles in the universe, or else we would have noticed these flashes.

单极子如同微型炸弹，能够催化聚变反应并引发爆炸；因此宇宙中绝不可能存在太多单极子，否则我们早就该观测到这些闪光了。

Many civilizations instead look for monopoles inside gas giants or neutron stars, but they have found nothing.

许多文明转而在气态巨行星或中子星内部搜寻单极子，但均一无所获。

But if monopoles have never been found, then how are they used in technology?

既然单极子从未被发现过，它们又是如何应用于技术中的呢？

They are created.

它们是人造的。

You can even create your own monopoles at home!

你甚至可以在家里亲手制造单极子！

It's home experiment time!

现在是家庭实验时间！

All you need is a particle accelerator the size of a planet, large enough to break the symmetry of the universe and create defects.

你所需要的仅仅是一台行星大小的粒子加速器，其规模大到足以造成对称性破缺，创造出宇宙级别的“缺陷”。

Pause the video for a few minutes and come back when you have the necessary materials.

请先暂停几分钟，等你备齐上述所需材料后，再回来继续。

Done?

准备好了吗孩子们？

Great! Now you can create certain special monopoles that are used to catalyze fusion reactions.

很好！现在你就可以制造特定的特殊单极子，用其催化聚变反应。

Make sure to be careful.

操作时务必注意安全。

Now for our next home experiment.

接下来是我们的下一个家庭实验。

This experiment will require the guidance of an S3 or higher.

本实验需要一位S3或更高位阶存在的指导。

Alternatively, you could ascend to S3 or higher now, if you are not already.

或者，如果尚不满足实验条件，你也直接飞升至S3或更高位阶。

Also, you need temperatures of 10 to the power of 27 Kelvin.

此外，你还需要准备1027K的高温。

We will give you another few minutes to do that.

我们同样会再给你几分钟去准备。

Are you done?

准备好了吗？

Hooray!

好极了！

Now you need to use you or your archailect's deep understanding of supersymmetry—aka SuSy.

现在，你需要运用古智体或你自己对于SuSy（超对称）的深刻理解。

Now you can create monopoles using the temperatures of 10 to the power of 27 Kelvin.

如此一来，你便能借助1027K的高温来制造单极子了。

But these are not monopoles like the ones before.

但此类单极子与先前提到的那些截然不同。

The ones before weren't stable; these ones are stable.

此前所述的单极子并不稳定，而眼前这些则是稳定存在的。

You can still catalyze fusion using these, but you now have access to new super cool weapons, such as bombs.

你依然可以用它们来催化聚变，但也可以用其制造全新的超级武器，比如炸弹。

These bombs are even better than antimatter bombs.

这些炸弹甚至比反物质武器还要优越得多。

They last longer, are more deadly, and are easier to control.

它们不仅贮存寿命更长、杀伤力更强，而且也更易控制。

They basically catalyze fusion really fast.

其原理基本上就是极快地催化聚变。

Antimatter bombs waste lots of their energy on muons, but monopole bombs do not have this flaw.

反物质炸弹会将大量能量浪费在μ子上，而单极子炸弹则不存在这一缺陷。

So buy our monopole bombs today!

所以，今天就下单订购我们的单极子炸弹吧！

Do not settle for a competitor's weak antimatter bombs!

切莫将就于友商那些威力孱弱的反物质炸弹！

Magnetic monopoles are basically tiny magnetic poles, so there's a much more straightforward use for them, magnetic fields.

磁单极子本质上就是微小的磁极，因此它们还有一种更为直接的用途：制造磁场。

Compact, strong magnetic fields are very useful.

紧凑的强磁场极具应用价值。
tips：compact译为“紧凑”，强磁场科学中心研制出紧凑型动态磁扭矩探测器。

They can be used to suck up hydrogen from the interstellar medium and launch it out again at very high speeds, powering spacecraft.

它们可用于从星际介质中收集氢，并以极高速度将其喷射出去，从而驱动航天器。
tips：巴萨德冲压发动机的原理。

Once you have monopoles, you do not need to go through the long process of creating them again.

一旦获得了单极子，你便不必再重复先前漫长的制造过程。

You only need a particle accelerator of 10 kilometers or more.

你只需一台10公里及以上的粒子加速器。

Just smash two monopoles together and you can breed more.

只需让两个单极子相互对撞，就能增殖出更多单极子。

Now you can have many monopoles.

至此，即可获得源源不断的单极子。

With all these monopoles, you can create a material known as magmatter, which is super strong and dense and can be used for a wide range of technologies.

有了这些单极子，你便能制造出一种名为磁物质的材料，其强度与密度极高，广泛应用于诸多技术之中。

Magmatter is everywhere, so much that the current era is often called the Magmatter Age.

磁物质无处不在，以至于当前的时代常被称为磁物质时代。

So what is magmatter?

那么，何为磁物质？

It is made up of tiny particles called magatoms, which are just like regular atoms but made out of magnetic monopoles.

它由被称为磁原子的微小粒子构成，这些粒子与常规原子类似，但由磁单极子组成。

These magatoms are made up of magnuclei and magtrons, which are analogs of nuclei and electrons in regular atoms.

磁原子由磁原子核与磁电子构成，它们分别对应常规原子中的原子核与电子。
tips：atom明显是“原子”，nucleon是“核子”，nucleus/nuclei（复数）是“原子核”，故译为“磁原子”、“磁核子”、“磁原子核”；magtron明显根据electron造词，但“磁子”这一译名已经被magneton使用，如核磁子（Nuclear magneton），故退而译为“磁电子”。

Both magtrons and magnuclei are made up of a magnetic monopole and a neutral fermion.

磁电子与磁原子核均由一个磁单极子和一个中性费米子结合而成。

Magtrons only have a magnetic charge of plus one or negative one, though magnuclei can have much larger or smaller charges.

磁电子仅具有+1或−1的磁荷，而磁原子核所带的磁荷则可以大得多或小得多。

Most magnuclei used have magnetic charges between plus one and plus 12.

实际应用的绝大多数磁原子核，其磁荷介于+1至+12之间。

Magatoms can form from both a combination between magtrons and magnuclei, or a combination between a positive and negative magnuclei.

磁原子既可由磁电子与磁原子核结合而成，也可由正负磁原子核直接构成。

However, most magatoms used are a combination between a magtron and a magnuclei.

然而实际上，绝大多数磁原子都是由一个磁电子和一个磁原子核结合而成的。

Magatoms can be 300 million times smaller than regular atoms.

磁原子的尺寸仅为常规原子的三亿分之一。
tips：经典的语病“小XX倍”，英文中居然也有这种表达。

Because of this, magmatter is extremely strong and dense.

正因如此，磁物质拥有极高的强度与密度。

Typical magmatter is 2 million times stronger than regular matter and has a density of 10 to the power of 33 kilograms per meter squared(cubed).

磁物质的强度通常比常规物质高200万倍，密度更是高达 1033 kg/m3。
tips：此处明显笔误/口误。

That's a lot.

那可是好大一个数字。

Magmatter chemistry uses different mechanisms than normal chemistry, and these mechanisms are very advanced.

磁物质化学所遵循的机理与常规化学截然不同，且极为高深。

Also, the temperatures required to melt magmatter are very high, so magmatter is mostly only able to be used by high transapients or archailects.

此外，熔化磁物质所需的温度极高，因而通常只有高阶超智体或古智体才能运用它。

Magmatter is usually formed into string or coatings, as too much magmatter in one place—such as in a magmatter crystal, amorphous solid, or liquid—would be dense enough to collapse into a black hole.

磁物质通常被制成细丝或薄层，因为一旦大量磁物质聚集起来（形成磁物质晶体、非晶体或液体等），其密度将直接引发坍缩，形成黑洞。

There are special types of magmatter, such as magcarbon.

磁物质也存在着特殊类型，例如磁碳。

Magcarbon is used to describe magatoms that form tetrahedral bonds with four other magatoms, just like carbon in normal matter.

磁碳指的是能与另外四个磁原子形成四面体成键构型的磁原子，正如常规物质中的碳原子一样。

These magcarbon atoms can be made into mag-polymer buckytubes and mag-graphene.

这些磁碳原子能够制为磁聚合物巴基管以及磁石墨烯。
tips：巴基管，即碳纳米管（CNT） 。

Normal matter doesn't interact with magmatter like it does with other matter.

常规物质与磁物质的相互作用，完全不同于常规物质之间的相互作用。

When someone puts their hand on a table, their hand does not go through the table because of the Pauli exclusion principle, which prevents the electrons in the hand from occupying the same space as the electrons in the table.

当某人把手放在桌子上时，手之所以不会穿透桌子，是因为泡利不相容原理阻止了手里面的电子与桌面电子占据相同的空间。

However, magmatter can occupy the same space as regular matter and can even pass right through it.

然而，磁物质却能与常规物质占据相同空间，甚至能直接穿过它。

Magmatter can even pass right through neutronium, one of the densest materials made out of quote-unquote normal matter particles.

磁物质甚至能直接穿过中子物质——这是由所谓的“常规”粒子所能构成的最致密的物质之一。

Also, magnetic monopoles do not have an electric field, so they do not interact with electrons.

此外，（静止的）磁单极子并不具备电场，因此不会与电子发生相互作用。
tips：此处不太严谨，磁单极子不具备静电场，但很明显，运动的磁单极子会发生“磁生电”。

However, magnetic monopoles can interact with normal matter through magnetism.

不过，磁单极子可以通过磁性与常规物质发生相互作用。

Diamagnetic matter and superconductors repel magnetic monopoles, while paramagnetic and ferromagnetic matter attract them.

抗磁性物质和超导体排斥磁单极子，而顺磁性和铁磁性物质则会吸引它们。

Something important to note is that magnetic monopoles tend to stay away from atomic nuclei and core electrons, since those are mostly all diamagnetic.

尤为重要的一点是，由于原子核与内层核心电子大多呈抗磁性，磁单极子往往会自发避开它们。

Another thing is that when magnetic monopoles merge into magatoms, they become magnetically neutral and usually do not interact with normal matter.

另一点在于，当磁单极子聚合成磁原子之后，它们整体呈现为磁中性，通常不再与常规物质发生相互作用。

However, magatoms and magmolecules may have magnetic dipoles, so they may still be able to interact with normal matter.

然而，磁原子与磁分子可能具有磁偶极（矩），因此仍有可能与常规物质发生相互作用。

Many magmolecules are specifically designed so that they will interact with normal matter.

许多磁分子经过特殊设计，以使其能够与常规物质发生相互作用。

Magatoms with different magnetic charges are placed about an atom apart from each other.

带有不同磁荷的磁原子以约一个常规原子的间距交替排列。

The different charges are called the dipole, and allow the magmatter to be anchored to ferromagnetic materials.

这些正负磁荷所构成的偶极，使得磁物质得以牢固锚定在铁磁性材料之中。

GUT monopoles can break down normal matter, but monopoles found in magmatter do not have this property, and so can be combined with normal matter.

GUT单极子能够瓦解常规物质，而磁物质中的单极子不具备这种特性，因此可以与常规物质结合。

Many materials are a mix of normal matter and magmatter.

因此，许多材料实际上都是常规物质与磁物质的混合体。

However, if you make a sheet out of magmatter called mag-graphene and pass it through an object, the nuclei within the object may hit a monopole and disintegrate.

然而，若使用磁物质制成名为磁石墨烯的薄片并使其穿过物体，物体内的原子核可能会撞上单极子而解体。

Electromagnetic radiation interacts with magmatter in different ways (than) in normal matter.

电磁辐射与磁物质相互作用的机制，迥异于其在常规物质中的表现。

Electromagnetic radiation has a magnetic field that can push and pull on monopoles.

电磁辐射产生的磁场能够推拉单极子。

This can produce gamma rays.

此过程能够产生伽马射线。

If magmatter is a magnetic conductor with free-to-move magnetic charges, it will be very reflective and reflect most electromagnetic radiation.

如果磁物质是具有自由磁荷的磁导体，它便会具备极强的反射性，能将电磁辐射全数反射。

Magmatter materials that are magnetic insulators, on the other hand, will be transparent and allow electromagnetic radiation to pass right through them.

而作为磁绝缘体的磁物质材料则会呈现出透明的外观，电磁辐射能够直接将其穿透。

So what is magmatter used for?

那么，磁物质究竟有何用途？

Magmatter is used to hold megastructures together through cables and braces.

磁物质能够制成缆索与支撑件，用以维系各种巨构的稳固。

It is also used to coat objects and protect them from damage.

它亦可用作表面涂层，从而保护物体免受损伤。

Since magastoms are so small—even smaller than atomic nuclei—they can be used for a very special type of technology known as picotechnology, which is basically extremely tiny machines.

由于磁原子极其微小（甚至小于原子核），它们可用于一种极为特别的技术，即皮米技术，制造极其微小的机器。

However, it is hard to get magmatter technology to work at normal temperatures, which are basically like absolute zero to magmatter.

然而，要让磁物质技术在常温下运作却极其困难，因为对磁物质而言，室温基本上等同于绝对零度。

However, with enough energy in the environment, these devices can even self-replicate.

但在能量充足的环境中，这些装置甚至能够自我复制。

Magmatter computing is way faster than computing using normal matter, due to the small scale of magmatter and, by extension, the speed of magchemistry and magmatter interactions.

得益于磁物质极小的尺度，以及由此产生的极高磁化学反应与相互作用速率，磁物质计算的速度远超任何基于常规物质的计算体系。

Finally, there can even be life made out of magmatter.

最后，甚至还存在由磁物质构成的生命。

An archailect in the Perseus Arm, in a system known as the Magvivisystem Hyperpolity, created eight biochemistries of magmatter life that live inside a torus.

在英仙臂的恒星系MVSHP中，一位古智体创造了八种生化机制各异的磁物质生命，让它们栖息在一座环形结构之内。

The organisms are all less than 50 nanometers in size, and the biosphere is diffuse to prevent singularities from forming.

这些生物的大小均不足50纳米，且整个生物圈的分布十分弥散，以防止坍缩形成奇点。

In 9310 AT, a ship from the Perseus Arm Defense Organization investigated the system after a mysterious megastructure was discovered there, and contacted the local archailect.

在 9310 AT，来自PADO(英仙臂防御组织) 的一艘飞船，在该星系发现此神秘巨构后，对其进行了调查，并与当地的古智体建立了联系。

The star was an A-type star which was surrounded by a Dyson bubble 0.3 AU away, which beamed energy to a J-node and the torus, which has a major radius of 2.4 AU and a minor radius of 1.5 kilometers.

此星系的中央恒星是一颗 A 型星，距其0.3AU处环绕着一个戴森泡，该戴森泡负责向一个木星脑节点以及环体输送能量，该环体的主半径达2.4AU，而截面管道的次半径仅有1.5公里。

Eventually, a civilization of softened magmatter creatures emerged in the torus and self-modified and provolved, creating thousands of clades.

由软化磁物质生物构成的文明在环体中繁衍生息，并自我改造和前演化，创造出数以千计的演化支系。

Since the torus is so expansive and magmatter life is so fast and small, the torus has a diversity equivalent to a Sephirotic empire.

由于环体内如此广袤，而磁物质生命的繁衍又极度快速且微小，环内的多样性，几乎相当于一个质点帝国。

They were even able to breach the first toposophic level.

它们甚至能够突破第一拓扑智识位阶。

To the organisms, the rest of the Terragen Sphere is extremely slow.

在这些生物眼中，地源地带的其余部分极其缓慢。

The torus is mostly peaceful.

环体内部大体上是和平的。

However, there was a hundred-nanosecond war in Copernicus 9385 over 15 cubic centimeters, formed by a misunderstanding.

不过，在9385AT的哥白尼月，起因于误会，曾爆发过一场持续100纳秒、波及15立方厘米的战争。

Essentially, the system was connected to the nexus wormhole network, and in 9642 they joined the Technorapture Hypernation.

后来，该星系接入了枢纽虫洞网络，并在9642年加入了TRHN（技术被提超国度）。
