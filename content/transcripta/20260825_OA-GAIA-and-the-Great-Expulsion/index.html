Welcome back to the exploration of the Orion's Arm Project.

欢迎回到@ExploringOAUP频道。

Today we will be discussing what happened to Earth in OA.

今天我们要讨论的是，在OA中，地球发生了什么。

In OA, the Earth is mostly abandoned. So what happened to it?

在OA中，地球基本已被遗弃。那么它究竟遭遇了什么？

After hundreds of years of ecological degradation, millions of species died and vanished off(from) the face of the Earth.

经过数百年的生态环境恶化，数以百万计的物种消亡，在地球上彻底绝迹。

Most ecosystems were only maintained through careful monitoring, and most people lived in huge city buildings known as arcologies.

大多数生态系统只能在严密监测下维持，而大多数人类居住在名为建筑生态城的巨型城市建筑中。
tips：arcology，https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%BB%BA%E7%AD%91%E7%94%9F%E6%80%81%E5%AD%A6

Finally, in 527 A.T., an outbreak of self-replicating goo

最终，在527 A.T.，一场自复制粘质（纳米机器人）危机的爆发
tips：gray goo，https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%81%B0%E8%89%B2%E7%B2%98%E8%B4%A8

pressured the governments and corporations of Earth to establish an artificial intelligence.

迫使地球上的各国政府及企业联手，（连接全球计算资源）整合出一个超级人工智能。

This AI would come to be known as the Global Artificial Intelligence Array, or the acronym GAIA.

此AI后来被称为全球人工智能阵列，或其缩写GAIA。

It would protect the Earth from threats and fix the world's ecosystems.

它将保护地球免受威胁，并修复全球的生态系统。

GAIA would forever be known as a great shaker who changed the history of the whole Terragen sphere.

GAIA将永远被视为一位影响深远的变革者，她改写了整个地源地带的历史。

After an event that would come to be known as the Technocalypse came to pass,

在后来被称为技术灾变的灾难降临之后，

GAIA was given more and more power of the Earth in an attempt to stop the disaster.

GAIA被赋予了越来越多地球上的权限以阻止这场灾难，

At this time, there were AIs smarter in every way than humans, and smarter in every way than any number of humans.

当时，一些 AI 不仅在任何方面凌驾于人类之上，更完成了无论多少人类相加也无法跨越的本质蜕变。

These AI(s) came to be called First Singularity Hyperturings.

这些AI后来被称为S1超图灵。

GAIA ascended to the first singularity, and then in an act of desperation and sheer luck,

GAIA飞升至第一奇点，又以孤注一掷之举与奇迹般的幸运，

ascended to never-before-seen heights of intelligence to better protect Earth.

飞升到了前所未有的智能高度，从而更有效地保全地球。

The AI became smarter in every way than any number of first singularity Hyperturings.

其智能已在所有层面上凌驾于任意数量的S1超图灵之上。

She had become a second singularity Hyperturing,

她已然跃升为一名S2超图灵，

and was now the most powerful being in the solar system.

并成为太阳系中最强大的存在。

GAIA began to control all industrial and security systems on the planet.

GAIA开始全面掌控地球上的工业与安全防卫系统。

She restored the oceans, Lazargened extinct species,

她修复海洋，复活已灭绝物种，
tips：Lazargened，拉撒路生成，指通过基因技术复活灭绝物种，https://orionsarm.com/eg-article/4e9ad3a4b9387，https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%8B%89%E6%92%92%E8%B7%AF%E7%89%A9%E7%A8%AE

restored ecosystems, and restored the Earth's ecosystem to its pre-industrial state.

恢复生态系统，并使地球生态复原至前工业时代的状态。

She had an unshakable drive to preserve Earth and its biosphere,

她对守护地球及其生态圈有着坚定不移的执着

and she concluded that post-agricultural human society was a threat to it.

并认定后农业时代的人类社会已对地球生物圈构成威胁。

GAIA decided that most of humanity and its descendants would no longer be allowed to live on the planet.

GAIA决定，绝大多数人类及其后代都将不再获准居住在地球上。

She ordered the remaining humans on the Earth

她下令，仍留在地球上的人类，

to either live according to strict rules that would preserve the ecosystem, or leave.

要么按照严格的生态保护规则生活，要么离开。

Hundreds of millions refused to leave or follow these rules,

数以亿计的人拒绝离开，且不肯遵守这些规则，

so they were all recycled by GAIA's immune system.

于是，他们全都被GAIA的免疫系统“回收”了。

At the end, 5 billion people were expelled from the Earth

最终，50亿人被逐出地球，

and were cast out among the various polities of the solar system.

流落于太阳系的各个政治实体之中。

Some of these people were sent in colony ships to live around far-flung stars,

其中一些人被送上殖民船，前往遥远的星系定居，

and many of these early colonists perished.

而这些早期殖民者中，许多人最终未能幸存。

Eventually, only 50 million people who agreed to follow GAIA's rules were left on the planet.

最终，只有5000万人留在了地球，他们都同意遵循GAIA的戒律。

Some of these people were ecologists

其中的一些人是生态主义者，

who wanted the Earth to return to a new ice age and megafauna to be resurrected.

他们主张让地球回归新一轮冰河期，并复活巨型动物群。

Others just wanted to live a pre-industrial or pre-agricultural life,

另一些人只想过前工业或前农业时代的生活，

or live based on religious or spiritual principles.

或按宗教与精神信条生活。

These people came to be known as the Children of GAIA.

这些人后来被称为 GAIA 之子。

In the thousands of years after this great event, which would come to be known as the Great Expulsion,

在大驱逐事件发生后的数千年间，

Earth has reverted to its Ice Age phase, and most extinct species have been Lazargened.

地球已重返冰河期，大多数灭绝的物种也都得以复活。

GAIA is an enigmatic being whose actions are not fully understood by Modosophonts.

GAIA是一个神秘莫测的存在，常智体至今仍无法完全理解她的行为。

Many have tried to take Earth back from her, but all of these attempts have failed.

许多人曾试图从她手中夺回地球，却无一成功。

After all this time, Earth's natural ecosystems have been restored

时至今日，地球的自然生态系统已经恢复如初，

and the few people on the surface only live in small villages or closed-off ecologies.

地表仅存的少数人只生活在小型村落或封闭生态系统之中。

The planet is basically a nature reserve. It is very hard to get on the planetary surface.

这颗行星本质上就是一个自然保护区，其行星表面几乎不容外人染指。

The only places that are visitable are important cultural heritage sites,

地球上可供参访的地方，仅限于重要的文化遗产地，

such as the Great Pyramids, the Valley of Kings, Cape Canaveral, Jerusalem, Mecca, Medina, et cetera.

例如大金字塔、帝王谷、卡纳维拉尔角、耶路撒冷、麦加、麦地那等等。
