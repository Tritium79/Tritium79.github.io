First, we will talk about **Sentience**, **Sapience**, and **Sophonce**.
> 首先，我们来谈谈**感知性**、**智能性**和**智识性**。

**Sentience** is the ability to **be aware** and to make predictions about the future.
> **感知性**是**具备意识**，及对未来作出预测的能力。

**Sapience** is the ability to think and solve problems.
> **智能性**是思考和解决问题的能力。
> tips：智人"Homo sapiens"

**Sophonce** is being **sentient**, **sapient**, and **self-aware**.
> **智识性**意味着同时具备**感知**，**智能**，和**自我意识**。
> tips：区分**aware**和**self-aware**。

**Sophonts** also have the ability to self-reflect and think about their own thoughts.
> **智识体**还具备自我反思、思考自身思维过程的能力。

Thank, ffx.
> 谢谢你，ffx。

The classification of **sophonts** is known as **toposophy**.
> 对**智识体**的分类被称为**拓扑智识学**。

There are various levels known as **toposophic levels**.
> 其中包含多个层级，即**拓扑智识位阶**。

**Baseline humans** are known as **modosophonts**.
> **基准人类**是**常智体**的一种。

**Modosophonts** are the lowest level of **sophont**, and they have not passed any **singularities**.
> **常智体**处于**智识体**中的最低位阶（S0），尚未跨越任何**奇点**。

**Singularities** are points in the **toposophic map** that, when passed by an individual, make that individual's mind and actions vastly incomprehensible to beings that have not passed that point.
> **奇点**是**拓扑智识图谱**中的节点，个体一旦跨越某个**奇点**，此个体的心智与行为，对于尚未跨越该节点的存在而言，便会变得极其难以理解。

Beings that have passed these **singularities** are higher beings and **transcend** their mental states and consciousness to the point of being incomprehensible to beings which have not **transcended**.
> 跨越这些**奇点**的存在已然属于更高层级，它们的心智状态与意识实现了**超越**，达到了尚未完成**超越**的存在根本无法理解的程度。

As far as **Terragens** know, you can pass six **singularities**.
> 在**地源体**已知范围内，至多可以跨越六个**奇点**。

**Sophonts** are ranked **S0** to **S6** on the **S scale**, for how many **singularities** they have passed.
> 根据已跨越的**奇点**数量，**智识体**在**S标尺**上被划分为**S0**至**S6**。
> tips：**Toposophic Scales**存在多种分级方法，**S scale**只是其中一种。

The process of passing a **singularity** is known as **Ascension**.
> 跨越**奇点**的过程被称为**飞升**。
> tips：《Encyclopedia Galactica》中，对Ascend和Transcend进行了区分，Ascend保留部分原有性质，Transcend完全抛弃低阶部分，但二者界限并不清晰，二者经常混用，本文也并未体现出此区分。

While **modosophonts** are not as smart or powerful as **transapients**, they can still be practically immortal and incredibly skilled,
> 尽管**常智体**在智力和力量上都不及**超智体**，它们依然能够做到近乎永生，并拥有非凡的能力。

and they can still transform and deconstruct planets and stars, design entire biospheres, and build **megastructures**.
> 它们依然能够改造与拆解行星和恒星，设计完整的生物圈，以及建造**巨构**。

Passed the first **singularity**, **sophonts** are known as basic **transapients**.
> 跨越第一**奇点**的**智识体**，被称为基础**超智体**。

These beings are capable of processing and problems solving thousands of times faster than  **modosophonts**.
> 它们处理信息、解决问题的速度，比**常智体**快数千倍。

They have mental abilities and senses thousands of times greater than any **modosophont**.
> 它们的心智能力和感官能力，均比任何**常智体**高出数千倍。

They can also simulate the minds of **modosophonts** in their own minds.
> 它们还能够在自己的心智中模拟**常智体**的心智。

Above them are **S2**, which are the **S1** as **S1** are the **modosophonts**.
> 接下来是**S2**，**S2**之于**S1**，正如**S1**之于**常智体**。

**S2** have the power of billions of **modosophonts**.
> **S2**的能力，相当于数十亿**常智体**的总和。

They can focus their attention on anything without getting tired or bored.
> 它们可以将注意力集中于任何事物，而不会感到疲倦或厌烦。

They can modify themselves and their thinking processes, and can solve complex problems automatically without even focusing on them.
> 它们能够改造自身及自我思维过程，甚至能自动解决复杂问题，而无需投以注意力。

They are aware of every tiny detail in the environment around them at all times.
> 它们始终能够感知周遭环境中的一切细节。

These advanced computational abilities allowed them to invent many technologies that would take **modosophonts** thousands or even millions of years to invent.
> 凭借先进的运算能力，它们得以创造出一项又一项技术奇迹，而常智体则要耗费数千年甚至数百万年的时间，才能取得同样的成果。

Next are the **S3**, **S2** are to **S3** as bacteria are to **baseline humans**.
> 接下来是**S3**，**S2**之于**S3**，犹如细菌之于**基准人类**。

**S3** requires so much substrate that their brains are the size of moons.
> **S3** 需要巨量的运算基质，其大脑的规模堪比月球。

**S3** is the transition from **transapients** to **archailects**.
> **S3**是**超智体**向**古智体**的过渡。

**Archailects** are the gods of **OA**, far beyond the comprehension of any **modosophont** or **transapient**.
> **古智体**在**OA**中宛若神明，远非任何**常智体**或**超智体**所能理解。

They are like forces of nature.
> 它们宛如自然伟力。

**S4** and **S5** **Archailects** require brains the size of gas giants or even stars.
> **S4** 和 **S5** 级的**古智体**，需要气态巨行星乃至恒星规模的大脑。

**Star brains** are known as **Matrioshka brains**.
> **恒星脑**被称为**马特里奥什卡脑**。
> tips：Matrioshka，俄罗斯套娃。

They have the ability to create wormholes and dangerous spacetime weapons that can wipe out whole systems in an instant.
> 它们能够创造虫洞和危险的时空武器，后者足以在顷刻间抹除整个恒星系。

Even individual parts of their minds are self-aware and **sophont**.
> 甚至连它们心智的各个部分，也具有自我意识，且其本身就是**智识体**。

They can do seemingly impossible tasks in an instant.
> 它们能在转瞬间完成看似不可能的任务。

Last are the **S6**.
> 最后一个层级是**S6**。

There are only 34 of these in the entire **Terragen Sphere**.
> 这样的存在，放眼整个**地源地带**，也不过寥寥34个。

These require thousands of megascale brains nodes linked together.
> 它们需要数千个宏尺度大脑节点相互连接。

These beings are so powerful that they can create new universes that can be accessed through wormholes.
> 它们强大到足以创造全新的宇宙，而虫洞即为其门户。
