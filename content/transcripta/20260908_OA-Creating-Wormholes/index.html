[译稿][待整理]虫洞制造指南（Orion's Arm Universe Project）（@ExploringOAUP）

8 Sep. 2026 / 丙午年 丁酉月 乙酉日

（翻译已完成，视频字幕需要找时间做）

Light is the fastest thing in the universe.

光是宇宙中最快的事物。

It is impossible for anything with mass to travel faster than it or even at the same speed as it.

任何具有质量的事物，都绝无可能超越，或仅仅追平光速。

But yet, Terragens are able to keep their massive empires connected.

然而，地源体却能让他们庞大的帝国保持相连。

So, how is it possible that Terragens can quickly hop from system to system in a matter of days and can communicate near instantly between thousands of light years?

那么，地源体何以在数日内于不同恒星系之间跃迁，又能相隔数千光年却近乎即时通讯？

The answer is wormholes.

答案即为虫洞。

What are wormholes?

何为虫洞？

Wormholes are spherical points in space that connect distances in spacetime.

虫洞是空间中的球状节点，连通时空中遥遥相望的两端。

If a mass greater than the wormhole tries to enter it, the wormhole will collapse into a black hole.

一旦质量大于虫洞的物体试图进入其中，虫洞便会坍缩为黑洞。

There are two types of wormholes that are commonly used in the Terragen Sphere.

在地源地带，有两类常用的虫洞。

Traversable wormholes and comm wormholes.

可穿越虫洞以及通讯虫洞。

Traversable wormholes, also known as Morris-Thorne-Kuhfittig wormholes, are large and are used to transport ships and large physical objects.

可穿越虫洞又称Morris-Thorne-Kuhfittig虫洞，体型巨大，用于运送飞船与大型实体物体。
tips：Michael S. Morris，美国物理学家
tips：Kip Stephen Thorne，2017年诺贝尔物理学奖得主，Michael S. Morris的博士导师
tips：Peter K. F. Kuhfittig，美国数学家、物理学家
tips：三者均为虫洞研究者，有大量关于虫洞的研究论文

While comm wormholes are used to transport data and information.

而通讯虫洞则用于传输数据与信息。

There are four main parts of a traversable wormhole.

可穿越虫洞有四个主要部分。

The first part is the Transition area.

第一部分是过渡区。

This area is the part where space begins to curve into the wormhole and it is 327 AU in radius.

此区域内，空间开始向虫洞弯折，其半径为327AU。

Because of this, traversable wormholes have to be located far from any stars or planets.

正因如此，可穿越虫洞必须设在远离恒星或行星之处。

When you get within 3 AU of the wormhole, you enter the second zone, the Vortex.

一旦进入虫洞的3AU范围内，你便踏入了第二个区域，涡旋区。

In this zone, spacetime begins to close in around the ship, and relativistic effects can be very harmful if you're not careful.

在此区域，飞船周围的时空开始收拢，稍有不慎，相对论效应将会变得极具危害。

Then the ship passes a layer called the Caustic.

随后，飞船将穿过焦散层。

It is some sort of thin layer of exotic matter or energy that holds the wormhole open.

它是某种由奇异物质或能量构成的薄层，正是它让虫洞保持敞开。

Without it, the wormhole would collapse into a black hole.

没有它，虫洞便会坍缩为黑洞。

Finally, there is the Throat at the center of the wormhole, which is where ships pass through.

最后，是位于虫洞中心的喉部，此即飞船穿行之处。

The whole travel process takes a couple of months.

整个旅程耗时数月。

The second type are communication wormholes, aka Hayward class wormholes.

第二类，是通讯虫洞，又称Hayward虫洞。
tips：Sean A. Hayward，英国理论物理学家，虫洞研究者

As the name suggests, these are mostly used for communication and are usually very small.

顾名思义，这类虫洞主要用于通讯，且通常极其微小。

Since they are so small, they can be placed near planets and stars.

正因如此，它们得以置于贴近行星与恒星之处。

They are extremely massive for their size.

相比其尺寸，它们的质量极为夸张。

A comm wormhole that is only 2 nanometers wide would mass almost as much as the asteroid Hygiea.

一个宽度仅2纳米的通讯虫洞，就汇集了几近小行星健神星的质量。
tips：健神星约 8.67 × 10¹⁹ kg。

These wormholes are essential as they allow fast communication between the different parts of an archailect's mind and between distant systems.

这类虫洞至关重要，古智体心智的各部分，以及遥远的恒星系之间，都靠它们实现快速通讯。

Wormholes can collapse if not monitored by a transapient.

虫洞须由超智体监控，否则便可能坍缩。

Even small instabilities can collapse a wormhole, causing a time paradox or violating causality also collapses wormholes.

即便微小的失稳，也足以令虫洞崩溃，而制造时间悖论、违背因果律，也同样如此。

So wormholes must be transported carefully in special ships called Linelayers.

因此，虫洞必须由名为铺线舰的特殊飞船谨慎运送。

Despite all these restrictions, wormholes have connected the Terragen Sphere through a giant web called the Nexus and form the basis of Terragen civilization.

尽管有诸多限制，虫洞依然织就一张名为枢纽的巨网，将整个地源地带连为一体，并构成地源文明的基石。

Without them, travel and communication between star systems would be much slower.

若无虫洞，恒星系间的旅行与通讯将变得极为漫长。

So, how are wormholes created?

那么，究竟如何创造虫洞？

Wormholes are created by advanced intelligences using structures called Weylforges.

虫洞由高阶智能创造，所用结构名为外尔锻炉。
tips：赫尔曼·外尔，德国数学家、物理学家、哲学家，规范场论的提出者

These Weylforges require a massive amount of energy that is gathered through collection swarms or reactors.

外尔锻炉需要的海量能量，由能量采集群或反应堆供给。

Weylforges are huge devices that in total can be millions of kilometers wide.

外尔锻炉无比庞大，总宽度可达数百万千米。

At the center of a Weylforge, there is a space called the Extraction Core which is around a meter wide.

外尔锻炉的中心，是名为提取核心的空间，宽约一米。

The outer part of the Extraction Core consists of rings made of magmatter spinning very fast.

提取核心的外围，由高速旋转的磁物质环构成。

Inside these rings is a strange object known as a Prism.

环的内侧，是一个被称为棱镜的奇异物体。

There's also another strange object known as the Extraction Kernel.

其内还有另一个奇异物体，名为提取内核。

Finally, there's the Seed, a small piece of magmatter 20 to 30 microns in diameter that is used as a target to form a wormhole.

最里面则是种子，一块直径20至30微米的微小磁物质，它是孕育虫洞的靶材。

Spanning millions of kilometers out from the Extraction Kernel are a series of accelerators known collectively as the Wormhole Production Module.

最外围是一系列加速器，统称为虫洞生产模块，它们从提取内核向外绵延数百万千米。

To form a wormhole, it accelerates particles at very fast speeds into the Seed, creating energies close to the Big Bang.

为孕育虫洞，它将粒子加速至极高速度，轰击种子，迸发的能量堪比宇宙大爆炸。

A vacuum fluctuation forms and a Caustic is folded by the Extraction Kernel to keep the wormhole open.

一场真空涨落就此形成，提取内核随之折叠出焦散层，支撑起整个虫洞。

The wormhole starts off microscopic, but it can be adjusted to a bigger size.

虫洞初生时只有微观尺度，但可扩展至更大尺寸。

Then the wormhole ends are transported to their desired locations.

随后，虫洞便启程运往预定目的地。

This is how a traversable wormhole is formed.

一个可穿越虫洞就此诞生。

But comm wormholes require much smaller Weylforges.

而通讯虫洞所需的外尔锻炉，则小得多。

Thanks for watching this basic guide to wormholes in OA. Goodbye, explorers!

（结语。）
