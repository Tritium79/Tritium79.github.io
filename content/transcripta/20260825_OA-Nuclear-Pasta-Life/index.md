Welcome back again to our exploration of Orion's Arm.
> 欢迎回到@**ExploringOAUP**频道。

Today, we are going to be talking about a form of life that is very alien to what we are used to. In order to find these life forms, we must travel to the **Einstein's Revenge Cluster**.
> 今天，我们要讨论的是一种与我们所熟知的截然不同的生命形态。要找到这些生命形式，我们必须前往**Einstein's Revenge星团**。

It is a cluster of neutron stars located far, far into space.
> 这是一个位于遥远深空的中子星团。

Now, to find what we are looking for, we must go deep inside of these neutron stars.
> 现在，为了找到这些生命，我们必须深入这些中子星内部。

Let's pick this one.
> 让我们选这一颗吧。

This neutron star is called KJEI-54-458945.
> 这颗中子星名为KJEI-54-458945。

We must find the **superfluid outer core** of this star.
> 我们必须找到这颗恒星的**超流体外核**。

This layer is filled with a substance called **nuclear pasta**.
> 这一层充满了一种名为**核意面**的物质。

In this region, the star's rotation and magnetic field are quantized.
> 在这一区域，恒星的自转和磁场都是量子化的。

**Neutron vortex filaments** carry a single unit of angular momentum each, while **proton vortex filaments** carry a single unit of magnetic flux.
> **中子涡旋细丝**各自携带一单位角动量，而**质子涡旋细丝**各自携带一单位磁通量。

These filaments interact with each other through attractive and repulsive magnetic forces, and form complex **knots** due to **quantum turbulence**.
> 这些细丝通过磁力作用相互吸引和排斥，并在**量子湍流**的作用下形成复杂的**纽结**。

Two filaments of the same type connect on contact, and this releases **Kelvin waves**.
> 两条同类细丝一接触便发生重联，并释放出**开尔文波**。

There are more **left-handed electrons** than **right-handed electrons** inside neutron stars, and this leads to **spin currents** of **left-handed electrons** traveling along **proton vortex filaments**, which changes the forces between filaments.
> 中子星内部的**左手性电子**多于**右手性电子**，这导致由**左手性电子**形成的**自旋流**沿着**质子涡旋细丝**流动，进而改变细丝之间的相互作用力。

These simple processes lead to more complex life-like processes.
> 这些简单过程逐渐催生出更复杂的类生命现象。

Filaments, when braided together, can store energy and information.
> 细丝编织在一起时，可以储存能量和信息。

**Kelvin waves** can also be used for signaling.
> **开尔文波**也可用于信号传递。

These braided threads can become capable of self-replication, kind of like our own cells.
> 这些由细丝编织而成的细线能够进行自我复制，有点像我们的细胞。  

When these threads bundle together, they can form complex life forms.
> 当这些细线聚集成束时，便能形成复杂的生命体。

Sort of like how on Earth, cells form more complex tissues and organs.
> 有点像在地球上，细胞形成更复杂的组织和器官一样。

There is a whole ecosystem based of these knots and threads, which is around 50 times smaller and faster than the one found on Earth.
> 这里有一整套以这些纽结和细线为基础的生态系统，其规模约为地球生态系统的1/50，速度约为其50倍。

One of the species found here is known as the **Hildemar's Knots**.
> 在这里发现的一个物种被称为**Hildemar之结**。

Producers in this ecosystem gain energy from **neutron vortex filaments** in the crust, since there is a different rotational speed between the interior and the crust of the star.
> 这个生态系统中的生产者从壳层中的**中子涡旋细丝**获取能量，因为恒星内部与壳层的旋转速度不同。

These producers, called **rotatrophs**, are anchored to the crust and gain energy from releasing **neutron vortex filaments** from the crust.
> 这些生产者被称为**旋养体**，它们锚定在壳层上，通过释放壳层中的**中子涡旋细丝**来获取能量。

Consumers absorb energy from the producers, either by being parasitic, directly absorbing from other organisms, or breaking apart other organisms.
> 消费者从生产者那里吸收能量，它们通过寄生从其他生物体直接吸收能量，或拆解其他生物体。

There is also a simple **chromatic biosphere** inside of the inner core of neutron stars, which gains energy from the temperature gradient.
> 在中子星的内核中还存在一个简单的**色荷生物圈**，它利用温度梯度获取能量。

**Hildemar's Knots** have a main body that is a **torus knot**, which can extend, elongate, and contract.
> **Hildemar之结**的主体是一个**环面纽结**，能够伸展、拉长和收缩。

This body is only around 2 to 4 centimeters at its maximum length.
> 其身体完全伸展时，长度至多也只有大约 2 到 4 厘米。  

Around this body, flexible limbs are coiled, which have many digits and cilia which can touch and feel.
> 身体周围盘绕着灵活的肢体，其上有许多指状结构和纤毛，可用于触摸和感知周围环境。 

They use these limbs to ensnare plankton and eat them.
> 它们利用这些肢体缠住浮游生物并将其吞食。

**Kelvin waves** and **spin currents** are used to send signals throughout the body of a knot.
> **开尔文波**和**自旋流**在纽结全身中被用于传递信号。

The nervous system of **Knots** is distributed along different points of their body, and the limbs are autonomous and can act independently.
> **纽结体**的神经系统分布在身体各处，其肢体具有自主性，能够独立行动。  

When **Knots** contract, their limbs can connect with each other easier and can rearrange and shift their minds.
> 当**纽结体**收缩时，它们的肢体更容易相互连接，并可重新组织与调整它们的思维。

Two individuals can link to form a **feeding mesh**, which allows the **Knots** to act as one entity.
> 两个个体可以连接起来形成一张**摄食网**，使**纽结体**能够作为一个整体行动。

Through this, individuals can share thoughts, memories, and personality traits.
> 通过这种方式，个体之间可以分享思想、记忆和个性特质。

They can even change the sex, thoughts, and personalities of the other individual.
> 它们甚至可以改变其他个体的性别、思想和个性。

**Knots** move through the star using the specialized movements through the flow of the star.
> **纽结体**以特化的运动方式，在恒星内部的流体中穿行。。  

They can also sense the turbulence of the area to determine what animals moved through the area.
> 它们还能感知区域内的湍流，从而判断有哪些动物经过这里

All **Knots** are born male, but can become female after reaching sexual maturity.
 > 所有**纽结体**出生时都是雄性，但在性成熟后可以变为雌性。

**Knots** mate by growing **threads** from their body and connecting them,
> **纽结体**通过从身体上长出**细线**，并将其相互连接从而进行交配，

then, they untangle from each other, and this creates an offspring which is attached to the mother.
> 然后，它们彼此解开缠绕，由此产生一个附着在母体上的子代。  

After ten days, it separates.
> 十天后，它便脱离母体。

**Knots** live around two years on average, going by Terran standard, of course.
> **纽结体**的平均寿命约为两年，当然，是以地球标准计算的。

**Knots** are good at coordinating with each other and forming tight-knit groups.
> **纽结体**擅长相互协调，形成紧密的群体。

**Knot society** is based of transferring personality traits from individual to individual.
> **纽结体社会**建立在个体之间传递个性特质的基础之上。

It is also based of the concepts of **orthodoxy** and **noise**.
> 这个社会也建立在**正统**和**噪声**这两个概念之上。

**Orthodoxy** represents the social orders and behaviors among the group that members are expected to follow, and **noise** represents new behaviors and personality traits that are not established among the group.
> **正统**代表群体中成员被预期应遵循的社会秩序和行为，而**噪声**代表群体中尚未存在的新行为和个性特质。

**Knots** make art using the turbulence of filaments.
> **纽结体**利用细丝的湍流进行艺术创作。

**Knots** can also manipulate ecological variables in order to make more plankton for themselves.
> **纽结体**还能调控生态变量，以增加供其取食的浮游生物。

As they understand ecology and mathematics well and can create accurate models of turbulence, they also understand that their star is spinning.
> 由于它们精通生态学和数学，又能够建立精确的湍流模型，它们也明白自己的恒星正在自转。

The **Knots** may have had more advanced technology in the past, as their biosphere was somehow able to colonize the six stars of the **Einstein's Revenge Cluster**.
> **纽结体**过去可能拥有过更先进的技术，因为不知为何，它们的生物圈殖民了**Einstein's Revenge星团**的六颗恒星。

The **Hildemar's Knots** were discovered in 9837 **A.T.** by the **Hrupta Investigatory Fleet**.
> **Hildemar之结**于 9837 **A.T.** 被**Hrupta侦察舰队**发现。

Data from the fleet shows that all of the biospheres share a common ancestry and diverged around 1.3 million years ago.
> 舰队数据显示，所有生物圈都源自共同祖先，约 130 万年前发生分化。

It could be that some other civilization created them and put them in the stars, or that this civilization copied a naturally evolved ecosystem on one star and put it in all of the other stars of the **Einstein's Revenge Cluster**.
> 也许是其他文明创造了它们，并将它们置于这些恒星之中；又或者该文明复制了某颗恒星上自然演化的生态系统，再将其置于**Einstein's Revenge星团**的其余所有恒星之中。

It is also possible that the **Hildemar's Knots** actually were more technologically advanced in the past.
> 也有可能是**Hildemar之结**过去的技术水平实际上更为先进。

Whatever the case, **Hildemar's Knots** are very interesting creatures who live in an interesting biosphere.
> 无论如何，**Hildemar之结**都是非常有趣的生物，它们所生活的生物圈也同样有趣。
